About & Contact

Three creative threads, one local story.

Colorful Creations grew out of a simple observation: the same people who buy flowers at the Coop are the ones booking face painters for birthdays — and both are looking for local art to hang on the wall. So we brought all three under one friendly name.

Founder story

It started with one too-many bouquets on the kitchen table.

What began as extra buckets of homegrown stems on a Plattsburgh porch turned into weekly runs to the North Country Coop. Friends started asking for birthday face painting after seeing photos from a neighborhood block party. Then a customer asked whether the watercolor over the mantel — a piece by a Clinton County artist — was for sale.

That was the lightbulb moment: flowers, face painting and local art aren't three businesses — they're one personality. Joyful, handmade and proudly local. Today Colorful Creations serves families, schools, libraries, cafés, salons and campus groups across Plattsburgh and Clinton County, and we're building the region's friendliest little directory of local artists while we're at it.

We still cut stems the morning of your event. We still learn every kid's name in the painting line. And we still answer the phone ourselves — because in the North Country, that's how business is done.
